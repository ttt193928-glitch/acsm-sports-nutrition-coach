# Study Paths

Use these paths to guide learners through the ACSM sports nutrition course materials. Adapt pacing to the user's background, but keep the order of concepts intact.

## 7-Day Beginner Path

For fat-loss coaches who need a quick map before deeper study.

1. Day 1: Course map and nutrition thinking
   - Read `course_index.md`.
   - Explain the difference between textbook content, teacher explanation, and coaching application.
2. Day 2: Carbohydrate basics
   - Use `module_01_carbohydrate.md`.
   - Focus on glycogen, training demands, and common carb myths.
3. Day 3: Protein basics
   - Use `module_02_protein.md`.
   - Focus on muscle retention, recovery, satiety, and common client mistakes.
4. Day 4: Fat and energy balance
   - Use `module_03_fat.md` and relevant energy sections in `module_04_energy_vitamins_minerals.md`.
5. Day 5: Vitamins, minerals, water, and electrolytes
   - Use `module_04_energy_vitamins_minerals.md` and `module_05_hydration_bioenergetics.md`.
6. Day 6: Body composition, metabolism, recovery
   - Use `module_06_body_composition_metabolism_recovery.md`.
7. Day 7: Fat-loss coaching application
   - Use `module_10_fat_loss_coach.md`.
   - Produce a review quiz and a practical coaching checklist.

## 30-Day Coach Path

For a coach who wants a working understanding before serving clients.

- Week 1: Carbohydrate, protein, fat.
- Week 2: Energy, micronutrients, hydration, bioenergetics.
- Week 3: Body composition, exercise metabolism, recovery, hormones.
- Week 4: Supplements, special populations, fat-loss coaching cases.

At the end of each week, ask the learner to explain:

- One key principle.
- One client-facing misconception.
- One coaching scenario.
- One boundary where referral or medical advice is required.

## 90-Day Systematic Path

For a coach who wants to study the full course.

- Days 1-30: ACSM sports nutrition main course modules 1-4.
- Days 31-60: Modules 5-8.
- Days 61-75: Review, case analysis, and supplement boundaries.
- Days 76-90: Fat-loss coach topic module, client communication, and mock consultations.

Every 5 lessons, create:

- A concept map.
- A 10-question quiz.
- A coaching translation: "how to explain this to a beginner client."
- A risk list: "what cannot be promised or prescribed."

## Default Teaching Output

When answering a learning question, use this structure unless the user asks for another format:

1. Core answer
2. Course source to read next
3. Coach translation
4. Common misunderstanding
5. Practice question
6. Safety boundary, if relevant
